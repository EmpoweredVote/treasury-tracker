## Milestone goal
Bring Ohio cities + county governments onto Treasury Tracker at parity from the single uniform Ohio Auditor of State "Summarized Annual Financial Reports" XLSX (Hinkle System, free, no auth) — general-government revenue by source + expenditure by function, per-capita, every figure sourced to ohioauditor.gov.

## Constraints
One uniform free source (ohioauditor.gov, no auth, $0); general-government scope only (`SOREACIFB_TotalGov` governmental funds — enterprise funds deferred); GAAP primary + CASH/MOD fallback (mixed basis recorded per-entity); no salaries (not in source); FY2016–2025; reuses the existing `treasury_sync_city_budget` RPC + never-overwrite guard; every figure durably sourced.
