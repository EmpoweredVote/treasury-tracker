<source_facts>
<!-- From 103-PA-IL-SOURCES.md — USE these, do not re-research. -->
- Statement: Governmental Funds *Statement of Revenues, Expenditures and Changes in Fund Balances* (printed p.~58). NOT Statement of Activities, NOT budgetary comparison.
- GF column = 1st of (General Fund | Motor License Fund | Nonmajor Funds | Total). Take the FIRST numeric token (General Fund) per row.
- Units = thousands. FY-end June 30. Window = FY2016–FY2025.
- URL pattern FY2016–FY2023 (hyphen): https://www.pa.gov/content/dam/copapwp-pagov/en/budget/documents/publications-and-reports/annualfinancialreport/june-30-{YYYY}-acfr.pdf
- URL pattern FY2024–FY2025 (LITERAL SPACE, %20, not hyphen): https://www.pa.gov/content/dam/copapwp-pagov/en/budget/documents/publications-and-reports/annualfinancialreport/june-30-{YYYY}%20acfr.pdf  ← SOURCES map MUST special-case FY2024+.
- Bookend ties (GENERAL FUND column Total revenues, thousands): PA FY2024 = 91,293,027 ; PA FY2023 = 95,231,042.
- Scope vs NASBO (TX trap): PA ACFR GF ~2.0× NASBO GF (federal/intergovernmental ~$42.3B sits inside the GAAP General Fund). Accept-and-relabel honestly via the GAAP basis label + source chip (confirmed at load in Plan 105-03). Do NOT silently double the node.
- Negatives: none in PA FY2023/FY2024 General Fund (investment earnings positive); older FY TBD at extraction → if any FY shows a negative GENERAL FUND category, rely on the P2 clamp.
</source_facts>
