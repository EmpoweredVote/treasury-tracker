<objective>
Build the two Illinois ACFR loaders on the proven v2.11 pattern — `scripts/processILAcfr.js` (GF spending-by-function, dataset_type='operating') and `scripts/processILRevenueAcfr.js` (GF revenue-by-source, dataset_type='revenue') — by downloading the IL FINAL audited ACFR PDFs FY2021–FY2025 into gitignored `_acfr-work/il/`, extracting the GENERAL FUND column of the Governmental Funds *Statement of Revenues, Expenditures and Changes in Fund Balances* via `pdftotext -table`, transcribing the verbatim categories into each loader's hardcoded data map, and dry-run-verifying every FY ties to its printed General Fund column total. Output: scripts/processILAcfr.js + scripts/processILRevenueAcfr.js, each dry-run-clean FY2021–FY2025 (or with any non-tying year logged + omitted, honest-hole).

<!-- From 103-PA-IL-SOURCES.md — USE these, do not re-research. -->
- Statement: Governmental Funds *Statement of Revenues, Expenditures and Changes in Fund Balances* (printed p.36–37). NOT Statement of Activities.
- GF column = 1st of (General Fund | Other Nonmajor Funds | Total Governmental Funds). Take the FIRST numeric token (General Fund) per row.
- Units = thousands. FY-end June 30. Window = FY2021–FY2025 (FINAL audited).
- Base dir: https://illinoiscomptroller.gov/__media/sites/comptroller/assets/File/CAFR/
  - FY2021: `ACFR Final 2021.pdf` (no Bookmarked suffix)
  - FY2023: `ACFR Final 2023 - Bookmarked.pdf`
  - FY2024: `ACFR Final 2024 - Bookmarked.pdf`
  - FY2025: `ACFR Final 2025 - Bookmarked.pdf`
  - FY2022: filename to enumerate from the comptroller ACFR page (same `ACFR Final …` family). If FY2022's audited file is not cleanly enumerable/extractable, OMIT it as an honest hole (do NOT substitute the interim file).
  - URL-encode spaces (`%20`) in the request as needed; store the human filename in a comment.
- 🔑 USE THE FINAL AUDITED FILE ONLY. The comptroller also publishes `… Interim … unaudited.pdf` per FY — the loader must NEVER point at the interim/unaudited file. SOURCES needs EXPLICIT per-year entries (not a single derived pattern) because the suffixes vary (FY2021 has no "Bookmarked", FY2023–FY2025 do).
- Bookend ties (GENERAL FUND column Total revenues, thousands): IL FY2025 = 78,342,927 ; IL FY2023 = 73,827,795.
- Scope vs NASBO (TX trap): IL ACFR GF ~1.5× NASBO GF (federal ~$22.1B inside the GAAP General Fund). Accept-and-relabel honestly via the GAAP basis label + source chip (confirmed at load in Plan 105-03).
- Negatives: none in IL FY2023/FY2025 General Fund (interest/investment income positive); older FY TBD → if any FY shows a negative GENERAL FUND category, rely on the P2 clamp.
