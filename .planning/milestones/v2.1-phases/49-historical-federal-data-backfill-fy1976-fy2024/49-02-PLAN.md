# Plan 49-02 — Function-lens backfill loader (build + dry-run verify)

**Mode:** standard
**Goal:** Turn the hardcoded-FY2025 `loadFederalFunctions.js` into a year-parameterized loader
that produces a sourced Function→Subfunction→Account tree for any historical year and the TQ,
with per-year anchors and the tiered reconciliation fallback. **This plan builds and dry-run-verifies
the loader; the real writes across the full span happen in 49-05** (so the first historical write
is gated there).

<threat_model>
**T-49-02 — Wrong-year or mis-sourced data written under an authoritative-looking label.**
- *Surface:* loader writes app-visible budget trees tagged with OMB source URLs.
- *Threats:* (a) extracting the wrong year column (off-by-one / wrong header) silently mislabels a year; (b) a year that won't reconcile gets written without disclosure, presenting an unverified total as official; (c) source_url points to the landing page, not the dated edition actually downloaded.
- *Mitigations:* anchor every year to its own Hist 1.1 total and HALT→fallback (never silently accept) on mismatch; Tier-2 writes always carry a visual-vs-official metric; persist the resolved `outlaysUrl` (the actual xlsx) as source_url; dry-run sample years before any write.
- *Block on:* high (data integrity).
</threat_model>
