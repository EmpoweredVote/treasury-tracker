Upgrade the **Tennessee** state node from NASBO operating-only to full State-ACFR GAAP by building two parser-based loaders on the NC mold — `scripts/processTNAcfr.js` (`operating`) and `scripts/processTNRevenueAcfr.js` (`revenue`) — downloading the TN ACFR PDFs across the full recon-confirmed window **FY2009–FY2025** (17 years, the deepest Batch-2 window) into gitignored `_acfr-work/tn/`, extracting the **GENERAL FUND column** (1st of General Fund | Education | Highway | nonmajor | Total) of the Governmental Funds *Statement of Revenues, Expenditures and Changes in Fund Balances* via `pdftotext -table` + the shared `extractGovFundGeneralColumn` parser, dry-run-tying every FY, then live-loading (NASBO replaced in place, revenue net-new) and verifying idempotency + cohort-untouched.

Re-verify each URL at load — recon URL details are unreliable (108 lesson); the exact tie is the safety net.

This is one of five independent per-state plans (D-08) — a failure or hold here blocks only TN, not the other four states.

Output: both TN loaders live-loaded across the extractable FY2009–FY2025 window (holes logged per D-01/D-05), NASBO replaced in place, Money In auto-enabled; `109-01-TN-LOADLOG.md`.
