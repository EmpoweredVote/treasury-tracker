## Milestone Summary

**Decimal Phases:** None in this milestone.

**Key Decisions:**
- General Fund scope chosen for all new CA cities (not all-funds) for apples-to-apples Money In / Money Out comparison
- Long Beach Port excluded entirely (separate government entity ~$760M)
- Oakland revenue deferred (OpenGov embedded chart format, no pdfplumber-accessible revenue data)
- Fresno + Riverside revenue deferred (no extractable GF revenue section in PDFs)
- Bakersfield operating scope fixed from all-funds (~$762M) to GF only (~$412-427M) during Phase 29-04 to match GF revenue
- San Jose FY2016–2020 skipped (older PDF format; FY2021–2025 loaded successfully)
- Oakland "General Purpose Fund" (GPF) ≠ "General Fund" — open budget shows all-funds ~$2.1B, GPF ~$800M

- ROADMAP.md showed Phase 28 as "3/4 plans / In Progress" — all 4 plans executed

**Issues Deferred:**

- San Jose FY2016–2020 (older PDF format) — older years skipped
