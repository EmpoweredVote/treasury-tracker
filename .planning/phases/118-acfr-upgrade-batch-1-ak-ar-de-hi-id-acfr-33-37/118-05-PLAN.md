Upgrade the **Idaho** state node from NASBO operating-only to full State-ACFR GAAP via `_acfr-work/gen_state.py` — `scripts/processIDAcfr.js` (operating) + `scripts/processIDRevenueAcfr.js` (revenue) — downloading ID ACFR PDFs FY2004–FY2025 into gitignored `_acfr-work/id/`, extracting the **GENERAL column** (1st of 3-4: General | Health and Welfare | Transportation | [Public School Endowment]) of the Governmental Funds *Statement of Revenues, Expenditures, and Changes in Fund Balances* via `pdftotext -table` + `extract_gf.py`, dry-run-tying every FY, then live-loading (NASBO replaced in place, revenue net-new) and verifying idempotency + 0 residue + cohort-untouched.

**THE ID RISK — MIXED UNITS.** Idaho's statements switch from whole DOLLARS in early years (FY2004 confirmed) to THOUSANDS in later years (FY2015+ confirmed), with the transition year unpinned in between. A single `UNITS` constant would silently misscale the wrong years by 1000×. Build a **reusable per-year units override**: the gen_state.py CONFIG accepts a per-year units map, the generated loader scales each FY by its own multiplier, and every stored figure ends up in RAW DOLLARS. Verify each year's units from its statement header ("(dollars in thousands)" note) or a magnitude sanity-check BEFORE scaling. Bookend guards: FY2004 stored = 2,314,491,978 (whole dollars, units=1) and FY2025 stored = 6,658,024,000 (thousands ×1,000). This is the first cohort state needing this — the generalization is reusable for any future units-transition state.

**3-way filename naming:** `{YYYY} Annual Comprehensive Financial Report.pdf` (FY2024–2025), `{YYYY} Annual Comprehensive Financial Review.pdf` (FY2021–2023, note "Review"), `{YYYY} Comprehensive Annual Financial Report.pdf` (FY2004–2020) — URL-encoded spaces, host `www.sco.idaho.gov/CAFRDocuments/`. Scope ~1.33× NASBO — accept-and-relabel honestly.

Output: both loaders live-loaded FY2004–FY2025 with correct per-year scaling, NASBO replaced in place, Money In auto-enabled; `118-05-ID-LOADLOG.md` with the load disposition + the per-year units map.
