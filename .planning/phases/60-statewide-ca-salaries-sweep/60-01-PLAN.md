# Plan 60-01 — spike gate + sweep wrapper

**Mode:** standard
**Goal:** Satisfy SC#1 (confirm GCC source coverage before any sweep writes) and stage the tooling: a
read-only coverage probe over the GCC statewide City ZIPs for the non-OC CA cohort, plus a new
`scripts/sweepCASalaries.js` that generalizes the proven `sweepOCSalaries.js` download-once pattern to
the non-OC CA cities. **No salary writes in this plan** — the dry-run gate lives here; the real sweep is
Plan 60-02. Satisfies SAL-04 (spike gates the sweep).

**Why a new wrapper (RESEARCH §sweep shape):** `sweepOCSalaries.js` reads its city set from the DB by
`county_id=OC_COUNTY_ID` and downloads each year's ZIP once (`:309-314`, `:331-382`). The per-city
`loadCASalaries.js` re-downloads the large statewide ZIP per call — unusable for 98 cities × 16 years.
The new `sweepCASalaries.js` keeps the download-once loop but selects the non-OC CA cohort.

<threat_model>
**T-60-01 — The spike falsely passes (claims coverage that isn't there), or the new sweep wrapper diverges from the proven OC sweep (wrong column indices, wrong Total-Comp formula, missing never-overwrite, or accidentally writes during a dry-run).**
- *Surface:* read-only ZIP download + CSV parse (no DB writes intended); new script authored from the OC template.
- *Threats:* (1) the coverage probe matches on the wrong column or a fuzzy name, reporting false coverage; (2) sweepCASalaries.js miscomputes Total Comp or uses wrong CSV indices (silent bad data later); (3) the wrapper omits never-overwrite or the --dry-run guard and writes during this plan; (4) the cohort query accidentally includes OC cities (double-load later) or counties/non-cities.
- *Mitigations:* (1) match EmployerName case-insensitively against exact DB city names and report per-city row counts + a city-scale total for eyeballing; (2) copy the CSV indices + Total-Comp formula verbatim from sweepOCSalaries.js (cite the lines) — do not re-derive; (3) --dry-run must short-circuit before any rpc call; assert zero writes by re-probing salaries coverage before/after the dry-run; (4) the cohort query filters `entity_type='city'` AND `county_id IS DISTINCT FROM <OC county id>`.
- *Block on:* high. Do not proceed to Plan 60-02 until the spike confirms coverage for the 10 other-county cities + a sample of LA County cities, and the dry-run shows zero writes.
</threat_model>
