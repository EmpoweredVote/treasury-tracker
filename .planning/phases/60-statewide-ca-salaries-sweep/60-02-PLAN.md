# Plan 60-02 — the statewide non-OC CA salaries sweep

**Mode:** standard
**Goal:** Execute the real GCC salaries sweep for the 98 non-OC CA cities across FY2009–2024 using the
`sweepCASalaries.js` wrapper staged + dry-run-gated in Plan 60-01. Idempotent, never-overwrite,
download-once-per-year. Satisfies SC#2 and SAL-04/SAL-05.

**Why this is safe (verified, RESEARCH §idempotency):** `treasury_sync_city_budget` keys on
(municipality_id, fiscal_year, dataset_type) and never disturbs a different data_source. Re-runs rewrite
same-source rows with identical data. The 60-01 dry-run already proved the resolved cohort is exactly the
non-OC CA cities (no OC, no counties) and that --dry-run writes nothing.

**Load shape:** one statewide City ZIP per year (16 downloads total for FY2009–2024), each processed for
all ~98 target cities — the v2.2 download-once pattern. Dry-run-confirmed in 60-01; re-runnable.

<threat_model>
**T-60-02 — The sweep loads wrong/duplicate data, clobbers Los Angeles's existing salaries, mislabels source, or silently drops most cities while reporting success.**
- *Surface:* `sweepCASalaries.js` writes salaries rows via the service-role key for ~98 cities × 16 years.
- *Threats:* (1) an OC city or a county sneaks into the cohort → double/incorrect load; (2) Los Angeles's existing FY2017–2026 salaries overwritten or duplicated; (3) rows written without the GCC source label; (4) a year's ZIP fails mid-sweep, leaving partial coverage reported as complete; (5) name-mismatch (DB name vs GCC EmployerName) silently drops a city to a false "gap".
- *Mitigations:* (1) cohort already gated in 60-01 (entity_type='city', county_id ≠ OC) and re-confirmed before the real run; (2) never-overwrite is by data_source — LA's non-GCC rows are preserved; verify LA FY2017–2026 rows unchanged post-sweep; (3) the write uses the verbatim DATA_SOURCE_NAME from the loader; (4) a failing year ZIP is logged and the year is recorded for retry — re-run is idempotent (the Phase 59 Riverside-style transient-error pattern); (5) the coverage results JSON lists gap cities explicitly so name-mismatches surface for review rather than hiding.
- *Block on:* high. Do not mark the plan complete on a partial sweep — every FY2009–2024 year must have been attempted, with any failed year re-run to completion.
</threat_model>
