<objective>
Source operating budget PDFs for all 6 remaining Collin County cities (Garland, Richardson, Wylie, Sachse, Murphy, Princeton), seed idempotent `treasury.data_sources` rows for each city's most recent fiscal year, then dry-run pdftotext against each PDF to record layout, sanity-checked grand totals, and a per-city routing decision (pdftotext-parser vs XLSX vs Haiku vs skip). | pdftotext output OK but no recognizable "operating budget" tables — only narrative text/charts | `skip` (likely summary brochure — Garland FY2026 risk) |
