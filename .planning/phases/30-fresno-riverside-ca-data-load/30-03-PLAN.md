<objective>
Build the Riverside biennial PDF pipeline — download biennial adopted budget PDFs into `docs/Riverside/`, write `scripts/extractRiverside.py` (biennial per-page FY detection, General-Fund-only filter at extraction time), write `scripts/processRiverside.js` (per-FY grouping + sanity band + RPC loader), dry-run, then live-load operating (and best-effort revenue). This is the City of Riverside, NOT Riverside County (Pitfall 6). Riverside runs after Fresno (D-09).

Output: `scripts/extractRiverside.py`, `scripts/processRiverside.js`, `docs/Riverside/*.pdf`, and Riverside General Fund operating rows (~$1.45B/FY) for ≥2 fiscal years in the DB. Revenue loaded if cleanly available, else documented as deferred.
