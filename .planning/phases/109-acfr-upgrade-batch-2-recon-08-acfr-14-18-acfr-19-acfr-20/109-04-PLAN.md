<objective>
Upgrade the **Washington** state node from NASBO operating-only to full State-ACFR GAAP by building two parser-based loaders on the NC mold — `scripts/processWAAcfr.js` (`operating`) and `scripts/processWARevenueAcfr.js` (`revenue`) — downloading the WA ACFR PDFs across the confirmed **FY2020–FY2025** window (6 years; pre-FY2020 not verified downloadable → deferred, do not attempt) into gitignored `_acfr-work/wa/`, extracting the **GENERAL FUND column** (labeled "GENERAL", 1st of 3+ with the Higher-Education major funds as neighbors) of the Governmental Funds *Statement of Revenues, Expenditures, and Changes in Fund Balances* via `pdftotext -table` + the shared `extractGovFundGeneralColumn` parser, dry-run-tying every FY, then live-loading (NASBO replaced in place, revenue net-new) and verifying idempotency + cohort-untouched.

**Two WA-specific traps: (1) FY2025's unique URL** — `FY-2025-Annual-Comprehensive-Financial-Report.pdf`, NOT derivable from the `CAFR/{YYYY}/ACFR{YY}.pdf` pattern; the SOURCES map must special-case it (and FY2020's `CAFR20.pdf` variant). **(2) The biennial-budget caveat** — WA budgets on a 2-year biennium, but the ACFR publishes ANNUAL GAAP figures per FY ending Jun 30 ("For the Fiscal Year Ended June 30, 2025" confirmed); the loader documents this and loads per-year. Also note the FY2025 PDF is large (~24 MB) and the "Investment income (loss)" column name signals possible negative years (P2 watch, ACFR-20).

This is one of five independent per-state plans (D-08) — a failure or hold here blocks only WA, not the other four states.

Output: both WA loaders live-loaded across the extractable FY2020–FY2025 window (holes logged per D-05), NASBO replaced in place, Money In auto-enabled; `109-04-WA-LOADLOG.md`.
