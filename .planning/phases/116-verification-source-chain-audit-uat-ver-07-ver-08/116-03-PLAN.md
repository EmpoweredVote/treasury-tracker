THE 5 STANDARD CHECKS at every ACFR anchor (unless the anchor says otherwise):
  1. Money In (revenue-by-source) renders with by-source rows.
  2. Spending-by-function renders with function/department rows.
  3. Basis label = State ACFR / GAAP basis (or the distinct pre-GASB-34 label where noted) — NOT NASBO.
  4. Source chip shows a real source URL + date; the link opens the state's ACFR/CAFR.
  5. Money In enabled on the node.

  - AZ FY2024 caveat anchor: renders with GAAP label + working source chip (the Drive-sourced year); FY2025 absent (not yet sourced) shown honestly.

  - AL anchor: Sep-30 FY-end state renders normally (spot the source_date if surfaced).
  - Deepening — CT FY1988: year selector reaches FY1988; row renders with the DISTINCT pre-GASB-34 basis label (visually different from a modern CT year's GAAP label — check an adjacent modern CT year in the same session).

  - Deepening — NJ FY2002: NJ (full-dollars) reaches FY2002; magnitude sane; GAAP label.
  - Deepening — MA FY2001: pre-GASB-34 label; and MA FY2002/2004/2005/2021 shown absent honestly (documented holes).
  - Deepening — WI FY2000: pre-GASB-34 label.
