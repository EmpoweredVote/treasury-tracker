<objective>
Fix the suspicious LA FY2025 revenue figure (~$44.6B). Root cause: the `LA_REVENUE`
data_source uses `actual_amount_column: 'revenue_collected'`, which sums all-funds actual
collections including enterprise funds (WATER AND POWER $17.2B, AIRPORTS $4.0B, HARBOR $0.9B)
— roughly $34B above the correct approved `revenue_budget` ($10.2B). Setting
`actual_amount_column: null` stops the loader from ever reading `revenue_collected`, so the UI
falls back to the approved `revenue_budget` for past years.

Purpose: Citizens see a credible revenue figure ($10.2B) that matches the scope of the operating
budget, not an enterprise-fund-inflated $44.6B that looks like a $34B windfall.
Output: Edited seeder, re-seeded data_source row, reloaded FY2025 + FY2026 revenue category trees
with no actual amounts.
</objective>
