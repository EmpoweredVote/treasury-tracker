<objective>
Fix the LA Money Out summary tile that shows $9.4B instead of ≈ $19.86B for FY2025.

Purpose: The seeder wrote a General Fund-only WHERE filter to the DB. When bulkLoadBudget.js
ran during phase 24 execution it read that filter and loaded only General Fund rows, producing
a $9.38B total that treasury_sync_budget_tree wrote to budgets.total_budget. The UI reads
budgets.total_budget — so the tile shows the wrong number. Fixing the seeder and re-running
the loader for all 10 fiscal years restores the correct all-funds totals everywhere.

Output: Corrected scripts/seedLADataSources.js; corrected data_sources row in DB; corrected
budgets.total_budget for FY2017–FY2026 all showing all-funds totals (~$9.4B General Fund +
enterprise-fund rows that bring FY2025 to ≈ $19.86B).
</objective>
