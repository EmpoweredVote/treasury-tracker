<objective>
Build `scripts/reconcileEV.js` (+ a shared `scripts/lib/evBankDeposits.js`) — the engine that turns the bank export and the platform exports into Empowered Vote's reconciled FY summary, and upserts it into `treasury.org_financial_summary` (from 75-01). It computes: (1) cash **balance** + as-of date and the trailing-3-complete-month **burn** → **runway** (D-01/02/03); (2) per-source **gross → fee → net** (D-11); (3) the **reconciliation variance** per source — platform net vs. the matched bank payout deposits — with an explanation, plus a list of **unmatched deposits** flagged for manual entry (D-04/05/07). Platform payout deposits are matched-and-excluded, never re-added as income.

Purpose: this is the truth layer EVVER-01 (Phase 78) checks and the Phase 76 view renders. Getting the deposit classifier + variance math right here makes "don't double-count" structurally true and produces the gross→net + runway figures the donor view needs.
Output: a shared deposit lib, the reconcile loader with offline tests, the live FY2026 upsert, and a docs section.
</objective>
