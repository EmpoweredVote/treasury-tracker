Prove the Phase 79-01 loader against a known-good target and pin down how much history Virginia actually publishes as XLSX — the two halves of VASRC-02 and the de-risk gate for the whole milestone.

1. **Prove:** a `--dry-run` on Alexandria FY2024 must reproduce the recon-verified totals (≈$863.6M expenditures / ≈$874.2M local revenue) with zero writes. If it doesn't, the loader (79-01) is wrong and must be fixed before any bulk load.

Years that exist only as PDF on apa.virginia.gov are out of scope (CONTEXT D-03).

Output: the committed FY→URL manifest + the loader reading per-FY source_url from it. No bulk load (that is Phase 80).
</objective>
