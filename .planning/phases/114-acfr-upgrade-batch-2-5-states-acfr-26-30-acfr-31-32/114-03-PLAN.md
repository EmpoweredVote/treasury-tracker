<objective>
Upgrade the **Utah** state node from NASBO operating-only to full State-ACFR GAAP by building two loaders on the IL template — `scripts/processUTAcfr.js` (GF spending-by-function, `dataset_type='operating'`) and `scripts/processUTRevenueAcfr.js` (GF revenue-by-source, `dataset_type='revenue'`) — downloading the UT ACFR PDFs FY2019–FY2025 into gitignored `_acfr-work/ut/`, extracting the **General Fund column** (1st of 6: General Fund | Income Tax [FY2025] / Education [FY2019] | Transportation | Transportation Investment Fund | Trust Lands Permanent Fund | Nonmajor Governmental | Total) of the Governmental Funds *Statement of Revenues, Expenditures and Changes in Fund Balances* via `pdftotext -table` + `_acfr-work/extract_gf.py`, dry-run-tying every FY to its printed General Fund total, then live-loading into `treasury.budgets` (NASBO operating replaced in place, revenue net-new) and verifying idempotency + 0 `data_sources` residue + cohort-untouched + UT municipal rows untouched.

**This plan resolves the recon's UT load-phase decision: load the GF column ALONE (option a), not a GF+Income Tax Fund composite.** Rationale: the phase's tie standard ("every loaded FY ties to its printed GF column totals") and the cohort-wide uniform mold (all 24+ ACFR states load the printed GF column of the same statement) both point at the printed column; a two-fund composite is a synthetic total no statement prints, and NASBO's broader survey concept is exactly what this milestone is replacing. The ~0.83× narrower-than-NASBO divergence — Utah's income tax is constitutionally earmarked (Article XIII as broadened by 2020's Amendment G) into a legally separate major fund — is documented honestly per ACFR-31 instead of padded over. The node total will drop vs the NASBO rows; that drop is honest and labelled.

Per 112-03's overlap probe, the UT state node is a clean NASBO-only node (no v2.5 custom-source residue) — the standard ACFR-replaces-NASBO supersede applies; the 15 v2.5 Transparent-Utah municipal rows are separate municipality rows and must remain untouched.

Output: both UT loaders live-loaded across FY2019–FY2025 (honest holes logged), NASBO replaced in place, Money In auto-enabled; `114-03-UT-LOADLOG.md` with the load disposition + scope-decision record.
</objective>
