<objective>
Upgrade the **Louisiana** state node from NASBO operating-only to full State-ACFR GAAP by building two loaders on the IL template — `scripts/processLAAcfr.js` (GF spending-by-function, `dataset_type='operating'`) and `scripts/processLARevenueAcfr.js` (GF revenue-by-source, `dataset_type='revenue'`) — downloading the LA ACFR/CAFR PDFs FY2002–FY2025 into gitignored `_acfr-work/la/`, extracting the **GENERAL FUND column** (1st of 4–5: General Fund | Bond Security & Redemption Fund | [Capital Outlay Escrow, FY2025+ only] | Louisiana Education Quality Trust Fund | Nonmajor Governmental | Total) of the *STATEMENT OF REVENUES, EXPENDITURES, AND CHANGES IN FUND BALANCES — GOVERNMENTAL FUNDS* via `pdftotext -table` + `_acfr-work/extract_gf.py`, dry-run-tying every FY to its printed GENERAL FUND total, then live-loading into `treasury.budgets` (NASBO operating replaced in place, revenue net-new) and verifying idempotency + 0 `data_sources` residue + cohort-untouched.

**This plan resolves the recon's LA load-phase flag: load the GF column ALONE (not GF+Bond Security & Redemption).** LA's GAAP General Fund is structurally unusual — **~99% of its revenue is federal Intergovernmental Revenue** ($22.48B of $22.78B FY2025, Medicaid/grant passthrough), while Louisiana's own state tax revenue (~$14.14B) is booked to the separate Bond Security & Redemption Fund column. The GF-alone choice keeps the cohort-wide printed-column tie standard (same statement, same column, all states) and follows the TX/MI/OK-class accept-and-relabel precedent for broader-than-NASBO GFs (~1.90×). The obligation ACFR-31 puts on this plan is prominence: the loader header, category names (verbatim — "Intergovernmental Revenues" will dominate visibly), and LOADLOG must make it impossible to misread LA's GF as state own-source revenue.

The mechanical trap: doa.la.gov uses **hash-based CMS media paths** (`/media/{hash}/...`) — per-year URLs must be enumerated from the two landing pages at load time, never guessed.

Output: both LA loaders live-loaded across FY2002–FY2025 (honest holes logged), NASBO replaced in place, Money In auto-enabled; `114-05-LA-LOADLOG.md` with the load disposition + composition record.
</objective>
