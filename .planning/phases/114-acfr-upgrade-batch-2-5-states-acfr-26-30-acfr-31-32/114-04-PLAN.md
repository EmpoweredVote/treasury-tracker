<objective>
Upgrade the **Alabama** state node from NASBO operating-only to full State-ACFR GAAP by building two loaders on the IL template — `scripts/processALAcfr.js` (GF spending-by-function, `dataset_type='operating'`) and `scripts/processALRevenueAcfr.js` (GF revenue-by-source, `dataset_type='revenue'`) — downloading the AL ACFR/CAFR PDFs FY2002–FY2025 into gitignored `_acfr-work/al/`, extracting the **General Fund column** (1st column in every era; major-fund lineup shifts — FY2002 had Public Road and Bridge Fund, FY2024 has ARPA Recovery Fund, GF stays column 1) of the Governmental Funds *Statement of Revenues, Expenditures, and Changes in Fund Balances* via `pdftotext -table` + `_acfr-work/extract_gf.py`, dry-run-tying every FY to its printed General Fund total, then live-loading into `treasury.budgets` (NASBO operating replaced in place, revenue net-new) and verifying idempotency + 0 `data_sources` residue + cohort-untouched.

Two things make AL unique in the entire cohort. First, **FY-end is September 30** (the only non-June state in this tranche; matches the MI precedent and the existing `FY_END_MMDD.AL='09-30'` entry) — `source_date = ${fy}-09-30`. Second, **this plan resolves the recon's AL load-phase decision: load the GF column ALONE (not a GF+Education Trust Fund composite)**. Alabama's constitution mandates a dual-budget system — the General Fund funds non-education government while the Education Trust Fund ($10,779,442K FY2024) is a legally separate major fund; NASBO's survey figure evidently combines both (GF+ETF ≈ 1.04× NASBO). Rationale for GF-alone: the phase tie standard requires every stored total to tie to a printed GF column total; a two-fund composite is a synthetic figure no statement prints; and the cohort-wide mold is the printed GF column of the same statement in all states. Consequence documented honestly per ACFR-31: the AL node total drops from ~$13.5B (NASBO) to ~$2.3B (GAAP GF expenditures) — expected, correct, and prominently labelled with the dual-budget driver.

Output: both AL loaders live-loaded across FY2002–FY2025 (honest holes logged), NASBO replaced in place, Money In auto-enabled; `114-04-AL-LOADLOG.md` with the load disposition + scope-decision record.
</objective>
