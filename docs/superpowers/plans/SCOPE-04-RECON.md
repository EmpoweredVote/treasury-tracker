## 1. The oracle, and its controls

`scripts/acfrPrintedTotal.py --column total_governmental` (Task 8). The column is located
by its printed **header** and then **tied to a real column** — a `/governmental/i` header
only counts if a money cell on a total row sits under it (within 40pt; adjacent fund
columns here are 50–90pt apart).

⚠ **Not "the rightmost number on the row."** Travis County FY2024 prints only General Fund
plus two nonmajor columns on its statement page and carries Total onto a continued page, so
"rightmost" would report **109,380,269** as Total Governmental when General Fund alone is
888,757,389 — a real number from the wrong fund, and nothing about the output would look
malformed. The selector **refuses** (exit 5) instead.

### Both PR #36 controls tie EXACTLY

| control | page | printed TG (expenditure) | PR #36 | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Cerritos FY2017 | 49 | `69,951,331` | `69,951,331` | ✅ exact |
| Lakewood FY2017 | 58 (continued) | `57,831,166` | `57,831,166` | ✅ exact |

Both also decompose exactly into their printed component columns, so the header-located
column *is* the arithmetic total:

* Cerritos exp `59,900,750 + 10,050,581 = 69,951,331` ✅ · rev `70,710,711 + 311,958 + 4,633,202 = 75,655,871` ✅
* Lakewood exp `46,962,212 + 181,200 + 7,286,202 + 3,401,552 = 57,831,166` ✅ · rev sums to `56,865,770` ✅

Austin FY2024 corroborates on a single-page statement: `1,347,127 + 1,534,052 = 2,881,179`,
read directly. `acfrGF.selftest.py` still reports **166 passed** — the default column is
unchanged.
